# Product Decisions

This section documents important product decisions,
the evidence behind them, alternatives considered,
and their outcomes.

## Decision Template

### Decision

What decision are we making?

### Context

Why does this decision matter?

### Problem

What user or business problem are we solving?

### Evidence

What evidence do we have?

### Assumptions

What are we assuming?

### Alternatives

What alternatives did we consider?

### Decision

What did we decide?

### Why?

Why did we choose this option?

### Expected Outcome

What outcome do we expect?

### Success Metric

How will we know if the decision worked?

### Result

What happened?

### Learning

What did we learn?
