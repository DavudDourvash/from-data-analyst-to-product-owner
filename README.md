# From Data Analyst to Product Owner

> A practical journey from Data Analytics to Data Product Ownership.

## 🎯 Purpose

This repository documents my journey from Data Analyst to Product Owner,
with a focus on applying Product Management principles to a real
Analytics Platform.

## 🧩 Product Context

I am working on an Analytics Platform designed to help analysts
explore governed data and extract insights without requiring direct
access to raw and sensitive datasets.

The platform aims to provide reusable analytical capabilities such as:

- Pre-built KPIs
- Data visualizations
- Filtering and exploration
- Analytical workflows
- Reporting
- Insight generation

## 🧠 What I'm Learning

- Product Thinking
- Product Discovery
- User Research
- Product Strategy
- Prioritization
- Agile & Scrum
- Product Analytics
- Data Product Management
